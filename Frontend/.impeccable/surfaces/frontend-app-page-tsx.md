# Admin console (whole app)

Mode: Operate. Audience: current-semester department leaders and VPs, the Design/Logistics/Media teams, super admins; Arabic first, much of it on phones. Job: run the semester, above all take an event from booked dates to published through the pipeline. Constraints: only real endpoint data; RTL first; light and dark; touch targets ≥44px.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the console is a Najdi house in Qassim; the pipeline is its front door. It refuses the SaaS default of a sidebar, KPI tiles and same-size cards.

OWN-WORLD: limewash ground (#F3F0E9) and raised plaster, mud-brick ink (#3A2A1F), one-pixel adobe rules; colour only on painted door plates that carry state: green done, ochre waiting on you, madder returned, indigo with a team, umber draft. Tarma triangles for progress, shurfa parapet atop the header, the calendar as bricks in mortar. Reem Kufi titles, Thmanyah Sans everything else, radii 4–6px, night adobe dark mode.

STORY: a leader opens the app and sees at once whose turn it is, books dates from the ochre door, fills in the request, and watches it move through Design & Logistics, Media and publish; a team member sees what is waiting on their team and finishes it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: phone home: shurfa header (logo, bell, avatar); the ochre "Your turn" door with the live hold countdown, tarma progress and Continue; then "Your department's requests" rows. The bottom bar holds Home, Pipeline, the ochre Book dates door, Events, More. Desktop: sidebar with the Book button; the your-turn door beside the brick-course booking calendar, then the club-wide stage board.

FORM: Mud & Doors, position 5 of 7 on the grounded list (assigned), seed key 22312cde.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
